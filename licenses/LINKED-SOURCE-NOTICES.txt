HPLIP 3.26.6 linked source notices

Official source: https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/hplip/hplip/3.26.6/hplip-3.26.6.tar.gz
Archive SHA-256: 5fb2235a9cb6ec07cb4786c4066b151359c301324d54aaabf69ae7eef35606b3

This build links the HP PCL3GUI2 engine and its core filter. License notices below are copied verbatim from the linked upstream translation units and their headers. The full upstream license collection is in HPLIP-COPYING. ErnieFilter is GPL version 2 or later; most other included hpcups units carry the HP BSD-style notice. Keep source/build modifications with any redistributed binary.

Local changes: explicit common/utils.h include paths for case-insensitive filesystems; model-specific factory; Apple compiler flags. The PPD uses an absolute filter path, A4 defaults, and RGB24 instead of RGBW to retain black pixels with this upstream RGB-only encoder. The raster encoder implementation is unchanged.
